How to read this analysis

Case type: Classified from title keywords, so these labels are estimates rather than official case classifications.

Duration: Time from filing to judgment where dates are available. Cases without a usable duration are excluded from duration summaries.

Priority score: A duration-based flag for likely criminal cases to help surface records for review. It is not a finding of delay, urgency, or judicial performance.

Estimated conviction appeal rates: Calculated automatically from criminal appeal case numbers, the apparent appellant/respondent in the case title, and the imported outcome label. Appeals dismissed are treated as convictions affirmed; appeals allowed or acquittals as set aside; partly allowed as modified. Ambiguous or unrecognized labels are excluded. These are estimates, not verified legal findings or trial-court conviction rates.

Classification coverage: Shows how many likely appeals against conviction had an outcome label that could be classified. Case titles and imported labels may be incomplete or inaccurate, and party names do not always identify the appellant reliably. Multi-judge bench decisions are attributed to the profile’s judge name and do not establish individual authorship.
